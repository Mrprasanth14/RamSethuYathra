Cancellation & Refund Policy

Please review our cancellation and refund conditions before making a booking.

1. Booking Confirmation

A booking request submitted through the RamSethuYatra website is subject to availability and verification. A booking will be considered confirmed only after RamSethuYatra confirms the requested service.

2. Cancellation Request

Customers who wish to cancel a confirmed booking should contact RamSethuYatra as soon as possible using the customer support details published on our website.

Cancellation requests should include the customer's name, booking details, service booked and scheduled travel date.

3. Tour Package Cancellation

Cancellation of a tour package may depend on the time of cancellation, accommodation reservations, transportation arrangements and other services already reserved for the customer.

Any cancellation charges applicable to third-party services may be deducted from the refundable amount.

4. Cab Booking Cancellation

Cab cancellation requests should be made before the scheduled pickup time.

Cancellation charges may apply where a vehicle or driver has already been allocated or operational expenses have already been incurred.

5. Room Booking Cancellation

Room cancellation terms may depend on the accommodation provider and the time at which the cancellation request is received.

Where a hotel or accommodation provider has a separate cancellation policy, that policy may apply to the room reservation.

6. Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility is determined based on the applicable cancellation conditions for the booked service.

Amounts already paid to third-party service providers, where non-refundable, may be deducted from the amount eligible for refund.

7. Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, RamSethuYatra will initiate the refund through the original payment method whenever possible.

The time required for the refund to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment gateway, bank or other financial service provider.

8. Non-Refundable Services

Certain services, advance payments, special arrangements and third-party reservations may be non-refundable.

Customers will be informed of any applicable non-refundable conditions before confirmation wherever reasonably possible.

9. No-Show Policy

If a customer does not arrive at the agreed pickup location or does not use a booked service without notifying RamSethuYatra in advance, the booking may be treated as a no-show.

No-show bookings may not be eligible for a refund, particularly where the service provider has already incurred costs.

10. Date Change Requests

Customers may request a change to their travel or service date, subject to availability.

A date change may result in additional charges if the price of accommodation, transportation or other services changes.

11. Cancellation by RamSethuYatra

If RamSethuYatra is unable to provide a confirmed service, we will notify the customer as soon as reasonably possible.

Depending on the circumstances, RamSethuYatra may offer an alternative service or date. Where an applicable service cannot be provided and a refund is due, the eligible amount will be refunded to the customer.

12. Force Majeure

Events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, severe weather, government restrictions, transport disruptions, strikes or other extraordinary circumstances may affect the availability of services.

In such circumstances, RamSethuYatra will work with the customer and relevant service providers to determine the available alternatives or refund options.

13. Third-Party Services

Hotels, cab operators, guides and other service providers may have their own cancellation and refund conditions.

Where such conditions apply, the relevant third-party policy may affect the amount refundable to the customer.

14. How to Request a Refund

Customers requesting a cancellation or refund should contact RamSethuYatra through the contact details available on our website.

Please provide the following information:

  • Customer name
  • Booking or reference number
  • Service booked
  • Scheduled date
  • Reason for cancellation

15. Policy Updates

RamSethuYatra may update this Cancellation & Refund Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with the latest revision date.
