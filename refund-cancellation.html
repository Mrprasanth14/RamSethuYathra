Cancellation & Refund Policy

Please review our cancellation conditions before booking.

1. Cancellation Request

Customers who wish to cancel a booking should contact RamSethuYatra using the available customer support contact details.

2. Cancellation Eligibility

Cancellation and refund eligibility depends on the type of service booked and the applicable cancellation terms communicated at the time of booking.

3. Package Bookings

Cancellation terms for tour packages may depend on the booking date, travel date, services reserved and costs already committed to third-party providers.

4. Cab Bookings

Cancellation of cab bookings may be subject to availability, driver allocation and other operational costs.

5. Room Bookings

Room cancellation terms may also depend on the hotel's individual cancellation policy and the timing of the cancellation.

6. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, the applicable refund amount will be communicated to the customer.

Refund processing time may depend on the payment method and payment provider.

7. Non-Refundable Amounts

Certain amounts paid to third-party providers or for services that have already been used or committed may not be refundable where applicable.

8. No-Show

Failure to appear at the agreed pickup location or failure to use a booked service may be treated as a no-show and may affect refund eligibility.

9. Date Changes

Requests to change the travel date are subject to availability and may result in additional charges where applicable.

10. Force Majeure

Events outside reasonable control, including severe weather, government restrictions, natural disasters, transport disruptions or other extraordinary events, may affect bookings and refunds.

11. Customer Contact

For cancellation or refund questions, contact RamSethuYatra using the customer support details published on the website.
