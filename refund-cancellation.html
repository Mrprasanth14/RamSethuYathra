01
OVERVIEW

Our cancellation approach

At RamSethuYatra, we understand that travel plans can change. We aim to handle cancellation and refund requests fairly while also considering arrangements already made for your booking.

Cancellation and refund eligibility may depend on the type of service booked, the timing of the request and whether arrangements have already been confirmed with service providers.

02
CANCELLATION

Cancelling a booking

If you need to cancel a booking, please contact RamSethuYatra as soon as possible using the contact details provided below.

Important

A cancellation request should be made before the scheduled service date whenever possible.

  • Provide your booking reference or relevant booking details.
  • Clearly mention that you want to cancel the booking.
  • Cancellation requests are subject to review and applicable service conditions.
  • Any amount already committed to third-party service providers may affect the refundable amount.
03
BOOKING CHANGES

Changing your booking

Customers may contact us if they need to change booking details such as the travel date, number of persons or selected service.

Changes are subject to availability. A requested modification may require confirmation from the relevant service provider.

↗
Contact us early

Earlier requests generally give us more opportunity to adjust arrangements before services are confirmed.

04
REFUNDS

Refund eligibility

When a refund is applicable, the amount will be determined after reviewing the booking and the cancellation circumstances.

Refunds may be affected by costs already incurred for transportation, accommodation, sightseeing, temple-related arrangements or other third-party services.

  • The original booking details may be required to process a refund request.
  • Amounts already paid to third-party providers may not always be recoverable.
  • Any applicable deductions will be communicated during the cancellation review.
  • The final refundable amount will depend on the individual booking circumstances.
05
PROCESSING

Refund processing

If a payment has been collected for a booking, any applicable refund request will be reviewed according to the booking terms and the circumstances of the cancellation.

The actual time required for the refund to reach the customer may depend on the payment method, banking institution or other payment-processing arrangements.

Please keep your booking information

Keep your booking confirmation, booking reference and payment information available when contacting us about a refund.

06
SERVICE CONDITIONS

Non-refundable or third-party services

Certain services may be subject to cancellation conditions imposed by the third-party provider.

Where a provider has already confirmed, reserved or supplied a service, the amount recoverable from that provider may be limited or unavailable.

  • Accommodation arrangements may have their own cancellation conditions.
  • Transportation arrangements may be subject to provider-specific rules.
  • Special arrangements or services may have separate cancellation conditions.
  • Third-party charges may be deducted where applicable.
07
SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES

Unexpected situations

Travel arrangements can sometimes be affected by circumstances outside the customer's or RamSethuYatra's control.

Examples may include severe weather, government restrictions, transportation disruptions, local conditions or changes imposed by service providers.

Such situations will be reviewed based on the circumstances and the terms of the relevant service providers.

Service availability

A cancellation caused by circumstances outside our control does not automatically guarantee a full refund. We will review the available options and communicate them to the customer.
