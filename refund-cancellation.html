01
OVERVIEW

Our cancellation approach

At RamSethuYatra, we understand that travel plans can change. We aim to handle cancellation and refund requests fairly while also considering arrangements already made for your booking.

Cancellation and refund eligibility may depend on the type of service booked, the timing of the request and whether arrangements have already been confirmed with service providers.

02
CANCELLATION

How to cancel a booking

To cancel a booking, contact RamSethuYatra by email or WhatsApp using the contact details in the Contact Us section. Please send the request as early as possible.

When is a cancellation effective?

Your cancellation time is the date and time we receive your written request. The applicable refund band is calculated against the scheduled start date and time of the booked service.

  • Include your booking reference or booking ID.
  • Provide the name and mobile number used for the booking.
  • Mention the service, scheduled travel date and that you want to cancel.
  • We will acknowledge the request and explain any applicable deductions before processing the refund.
  • Please do not treat a booking as cancelled until you receive our written acknowledgement.
03
BOOKING CHANGES

Changing your booking

Customers may contact us if they need to change booking details such as the travel date, number of persons or selected service.

Changes are subject to availability. A requested modification may require confirmation from the relevant service provider.

↗
Contact us early

Earlier requests generally give us more opportunity to adjust arrangements before services are confirmed.

04
REFUNDS

Cancellation refund schedule

For cancellations initiated by the customer, the following schedule applies to the eligible amount paid for the cancelled service:

Refund schedule
  • 7 days or more before the service: 90% refund.
  • 3 to 6 days before the service: 50% refund.
  • 0 to 2 days before the service: No refund.

The same schedule applies to advance payments. The refund is calculated on the amount actually received by RamSethuYatra for the cancelled service.

Where a hotel, cab operator or other service provider has already charged a non-refundable amount, that charge may be deducted only where the relevant condition was clearly disclosed to you before you confirmed or paid for the booking. We will explain the calculation and identify any such deduction.

If only one part of a combined booking is cancelled, the refund will be calculated for the cancelled service or portion, taking account of any disclosed provider charges. Services already used or completed are not refundable.

05
PROCESSING

Refund processing time

After we review and approve a refund, RamSethuYatra will initiate it to the original payment method, wherever possible, within 7–10 business days.

The time taken for the amount to appear in your account may also depend on your bank, card issuer, UPI app or payment provider. Bank holidays and payment-provider delays may extend the time before the amount is credited.

Keep your booking and payment details

Please retain your booking confirmation, booking ID and payment reference. If the refund has not appeared after the stated processing period, contact us with these details.

06
SERVICE CONDITIONS

Non-refundable and third-party charges

Some accommodation, transport, ticketing or other arrangements may be supplied by independent service providers and may carry their own cancellation conditions.

  • Any provider-specific non-refundable charge must be disclosed before you confirm or pay for the booking.
  • Only applicable, disclosed and non-recoverable provider charges may be deducted from an otherwise eligible refund.
  • We will communicate the basis of any deduction and, where available, provide details of the provider charge.
  • Services already used, completed or missed because the customer did not arrive at the agreed time are not refundable, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

If a booking has special cancellation terms, those terms will be shown to you before confirmation. They will not be changed retrospectively for a booking already confirmed.

07
SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES

When RamSethuYatra cancels or travel is disrupted

If RamSethuYatra cancels

If RamSethuYatra cancels a confirmed service and cannot provide the service as agreed, you may choose either:

  • A full refund of the amount paid to RamSethuYatra for the cancelled service; or
  • An alternative arrangement, only if you agree to it.

An approved refund in this situation will be initiated within 7–10 business days. Any refund for a separate service supplied directly by another provider will be subject to that provider's terms, which will be explained to you.

Unexpected events

Severe weather, government restrictions, transport disruption, safety concerns or other events outside the parties' reasonable control may affect travel arrangements. We will contact affected customers and explain available options, which may include rescheduling, an alternative service or a refund based on the services that can no longer be provided and any applicable disclosed provider terms.

Important

An unexpected event does not automatically mean that every part of a combined booking is refundable. We will review the affected services, explain the calculation and communicate the available options. Nothing in this policy limits rights that customers have under applicable law.
