Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RamSethuYatra collects, uses and protects information provided by customers while using our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the service used, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Number of persons
  • Travel date
  • Pickup location
  • Booking details
  • Room or cab booking information

3. Account Information

Customers may create an account using Firebase Authentication.

Password authentication is handled by the authentication service. RamSethuYatra does not intentionally store customer passwords in its booking database.

4. How We Use Information

Customer information may be used to:

  • Process booking requests.
  • Contact customers regarding bookings.
  • Provide requested tourism services.
  • Confirm or modify bookings.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain booking records.
  • Improve our website and services.

5. Firebase

Our website uses Firebase services for authentication and database functionality.

Information submitted through the website may therefore be processed and stored using Firebase infrastructure.

6. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell customer personal information.

Information may be shared with relevant service providers when necessary to provide a service requested by the customer, such as transportation or accommodation.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Customer Account

Customers are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential and should notify us if they believe their account has been compromised.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website or third-party services used by the website may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security and analytics.

10. Data Retention

Booking and account information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for providing services, maintaining records, resolving disputes and meeting applicable legal or business requirements.

11. Customer Requests

Customers may contact us regarding questions about their personal information or account information.

12. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact RamSethuYatra using the contact information provided on our website.
